## English

**I'll look at your site once, free. You get a reply within 24 hours.**

**Why free.** I haven't succeeded. ctofconverter.com is my own failed case: I shipped the indexing fix, and the traffic did not come back. All the raw data is public — read it first, then decide whether you want me to look at yours.

**You give:** a domain.
**I give:** one diagnostic review, answered within 24 hours.
**I don't give:** ranking promises, backlinks, or written content.

**Start with the post-mortem** (full write-up + raw data): **[TODO: link — post not published yet]**
**Raw ledger:** https://github.com/Jovceo/ctofconverter/blob/main/ledger/README.md

**How to book.** Open an issue: https://github.com/Jovceo/ctofconverter/issues/new
Put your domain in the title. **Your issue is public** — check that you're fine with that before posting.

**Terms.**

- First 5 reviews free. After that, **¥300–800** (≈ $40–110).
- Scope: same line as ctofconverter.com — temperature / unit-conversion tool sites.
- "Within 24 hours" means you get a **reply**, not a finished fix.
- **Privacy split:** reviews 1–5 (free) default to a **public** issue. From review 6 on (paid), you may choose a private channel instead — your domain does not have to be public.
